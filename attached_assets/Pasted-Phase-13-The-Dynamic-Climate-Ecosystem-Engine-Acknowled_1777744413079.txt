Phase 13: The Dynamic Climate & Ecosystem Engine
Acknowledge this prompt. We are implementing an autonomous seasonal cycle and a dynamic weather particle system. Append this logic into the existing Phase 12 engine without breaking the isometric rendering or dropping the mobile framerate.

1. The Seasonal Macro-Cycle:

Map the existing game months to Seasons: (Mar-May: Spring, Jun-Aug: Summer, Sep-Nov: Autumn, Dec-Feb: Winter).

Visual Overlays: Apply a subtle fullscreen canvas color tint based on the season to shift the atmosphere (Spring: slight pink/green tint; Summer: bright/warm yellow tint; Autumn: orange/brown tint; Winter: cool blue/grey tint).

Mechanical Impact:

Spring/Autumn: Farmer food yield is multiplied by 1.5x (Harvest/Planting).

Winter: Farmer food yield drops to 0. NPCs suffer a constant Mood drain due to the cold, which is doubled if their Safety is low (homeless).

2. The Weather Micro-Cycle (State Machine):

Create a currentWeather state (CLEAR, RAIN, SNOW, THUNDERSTORM).

Every new game-day, calculate a new weather state based on the season (e.g., Snow is only possible in Winter; Thunderstorms are common in Summer; Rain is common in Spring).

3. Lightweight Particle Engine (Visuals):

Build a highly optimized particle array rendered over the dynamic entity layer. Cap particles at 200 maximum to ensure 60 FPS on mobile.

Rain: Fast, steeply angled semi-transparent blue/white lines (ctx.beginPath, ctx.lineTo).

Snow: Slow, drifting white circles (ctx.arc) with a slight sine-wave wobble on the x-axis.

Thunderstorm: Rain particles + a 1% chance every tick to trigger a 'Lightning Flash' (momentarily filling the screen with an 80% opacity white overlay) followed by a delayed, deep rumble sound (if audio is enabled, otherwise just the flash).

4. Deep Logic Integration:

Wet & Miserable: If it is Rain or Snow, NPC movement speed drops by 10%, and their Mood drain accelerates slightly unless they are inside a building.

Lightning Strikes: During a Thunderstorm, there is a very rare chance for lightning to strike a random tile. If an NPC is adjacent, their Mood instantly drops to 0 (Traumatized) and they flee.
